# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps: being welcoming and respectful, accepting constructive
criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy
towards others.

Behaviour that does not: sexualised language or imagery, trolling or insulting
comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct
which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by
opening an issue at
<https://github.com/jongsu-king/nmr-analyzer/issues> or by contacting the
maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and
will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is
obliged to respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.
